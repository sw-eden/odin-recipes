Chips Masala Recipe

Description

Simple recipe for makinfg chips masala.

Ingredients

  • 3 large (cut into fries) Potatoes
  • 500 ml (for frying) Vegetable oil
  • 1 small (sliced) Red onion
  • 1 medium (sliced) Tomato
  • 1 tbsp (chopped) Fresh coriander
  • 2 tbsp Tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 1 tsp Paprika
  • 2 cloves (minced) Garlic
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1/2 tsp Black pepper

Steps

  1. Peel and cut potatoes into fries. Rinse and pat dry.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and deep fry the potatoes until golden and crispy. Drain excess oil.
  3. In another pan, sauté garlic and onions in a little oil until soft.
  4. Add tomato sauce, chili powder, paprika, salt, and pepper. Stir well.
  5. Add the fried chips to the pan and toss to coat evenly with the masala sauce.>/li>
  6. Garnish with chopped coriander.
  7. Serve hot with sliced onions and tomatoes on the side.
