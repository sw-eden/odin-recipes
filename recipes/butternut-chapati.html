Butternut Chapati Recipe

Description

Simple recipe for making simple savory butternut chapatis.Makes a serving of two.

Ingredients

  • 200g steamed butternut
  • 250 g All-purpose flour
  • 1 lt Oil
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 500 ml Warm water

Steps

  1. Mash steamed butternut until smooth.
  2. Add oil and salt to the mashed butternut and mix well.
  3. Gradually add flour, kneading as you go.
  4. Add warm water bit by bit, kneading to form a soft dough.
  5. Knead until smooth, then let the dough rest for at least 30 minutes.
  6. Divide dough, roll into flat rounds.
  7. Cook each chapatti on a hot pan until golden on both sides.
  8. Served hot, you can take it with tea, beans or stew.
